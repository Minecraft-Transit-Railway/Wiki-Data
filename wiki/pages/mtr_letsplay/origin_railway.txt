**Origin Railway** ([[wp>Chinese_language|Chinese]]: 原生鐵路), created by OriginRailway (also known as akivn), is a railway mainly serving the south-west part of the server. It features many stations and trains dedicated to an anime/game character.

===== Railways =====

There are three main types of railways:

  - **P-lines**, which are similar to Metros
  - **Rikka West Rail**, a sub-company mainly serving the west of Rikkashi
  - **LRTs**, which are similar to Light Rail
